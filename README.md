# 🎭 AI vs Human

**Can You Tell Who's Really Thinking?**

An interactive AI-powered mini app that challenges players to distinguish between AI-generated and human-written responses.

Developed by **GOYAL DIGITAL LABS™**.

---

## 📖 Overview

AI vs Human is an educational mini app designed to improve critical thinking in the age of Generative AI.

Players are presented with two realistic writing samples in each round and must identify which one was generated by AI. The experience demonstrates that writing style alone is often not enough to determine authorship.

Rather than encouraging users to "beat AI," the project promotes analytical thinking and AI literacy.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 6 interactive challenge rounds
- 🤖 AI vs Human writing comparisons
- 🧠 Critical thinking focused gameplay
- 📊 Detailed result analysis
- 📈 Accuracy score and round-by-round breakdown
- 🔄 Replay functionality
- 📱 Mobile-first interface
- 🚀 Built for learning through interaction

## 🎯 Learning Objective

The objective of this project is not to outperform AI.

Instead, it encourages users to think critically and understand that both humans and AI can produce convincing writing. Meaningful evaluation requires analysing structure, reasoning, and context rather than relying only on writing style.
